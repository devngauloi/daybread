Daybread — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Daybread shows a Bible verse in the app, in a home-screen widget and on your lock screen. It is published by ikistudio.

What we collect

Nothing. Daybread has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. The app does not request internet access: the whole Bible (World English Bible, public domain) is inside the app.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (topics, text size, lock-screen background, whether the reminder is on) are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app. The lock-screen picture is drawn on your phone and set as your lock-screen wallpaper only if you switch that on.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: policies.google.com/privacy. The app asks Google Play only whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, the app shows one local reminder two days before the trial ends. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

Permissions

  • Set wallpaper — to change your lock screen each day, only when you switch it on.
  • Notifications — for the trial reminder, only if you allow it.

Children

Daybread is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
