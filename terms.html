Daybread — Terms of Use

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The app

Daybread shows verses from the World English Bible, which is in the public domain (eBible.org). Reading the Bible in the app is free. The home-screen widget and the daily lock screen need a subscription.

Subscriptions

  • Yearly: 7 days free, then charged once a year. Monthly: charged once a month, no free trial. Prices are shown in the app and in Google Play before you confirm, and may differ by country.
  • Payment is charged to your Google Play account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (free trial included).
  • If you allow notifications, we remind you two days before a free trial turns into a paid year.
  • Cancel anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from Daybread → Settings → "Manage or cancel in Google Play".
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

No warranty

The app is provided as is. We do our best to show the text exactly as the World English Bible publishes it; tell us if you find a difference.
